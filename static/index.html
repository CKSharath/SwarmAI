How the colony works

This page explains the algorithm and has small demos. The demos are self-contained: they never touch the running simulation.

1. The chunk graph

The PDF is cut into short chunks. Each chunk is a node. An edge joins two chunks that are close in meaning (8 nearest neighbours) or next to each other in the text. Edges carry a weight w (similarity, 0.6 for reading-order links) and a pheromone level τ.

Gold line: one ant's walk through the graph.

2. Ants

An ant is a short random walk: it starts at a chunk chosen in proportion to its relevance to your question, then takes up to 6 steps, never visiting a chunk twice. Each round releases 24 ants; at most 14 rounds run.

3. Choosing the next chunk

P(i→j) = τijα · ηjβ · wij ÷ Σk τikα · ηkβ · wik

τ (pheromone) is the colony's memory: how much earlier good ants used this edge. η (heuristic) is how relevant the destination chunk is to your question (half keyword score, half embedding similarity). α and β set how much each counts: with α=0 the ant ignores memory; with β=0 it ignores relevance. This project uses α=1, β=2.

Try it: probability calculator
Three candidate chunks A, B, C. Move the sliders and watch the probabilities.

4. Exploration versus exploitation

Following the formula alone would make ants copy each other and lock onto early trails (exploitation). So with probability ε = 10% an ant ignores the formula and picks a random unvisited neighbour (exploration). The effective probability is (1−ε)·P + ε/n. The calculator above has an ε slider for this.

5. Depositing pheromone

After each round, the best quarter of ants (the elite, ranked by the sum of η along their path) lay pheromone on every edge they used: Δτ = (path quality) ÷ (path length). Pheromone is kept between 0.1 and 6 so no trail can vanish completely or grow without limit.

6. Evaporation

τ ← (1 − ρ) · τ (then deposits are added)

Every round 35% (ρ) of every trail evaporates. Edges that stop being useful fade, so the colony can change its mind.

Try it: one edge over 14 rounds
The edge is used by elite ants for the first N rounds, then abandoned.

7. Finding the best path and converging

Each chunk also collects "heat" from the elite ants that pass through it. The hottest chunks hold the evidence for your answer. The colony converges when the five hottest chunks are the same for 3 rounds in a row (checked from round 5). If that never happens it stops at round 14. The Colony Monitor tab charts this for your own run.
